# Build a Mobile-First Driver & Warehouse Arrival Coordination Platform

Act as a Principal Product Architect, Principal Mobile Engineer, Senior UX Designer, and Enterprise Logistics Software Engineer.

Build a production-grade mobile-first platform focused on solving warehouse and facility arrival, check-in, loading, unloading, dock assignment, and departure workflows.

This is NOT a fleet tracking platform.

Drivers already use systems like Samsara, Motive, Geotab, and ELD solutions for vehicle tracking.

This platform focuses on the operational gap between:

Driver Arrival
→ Warehouse Check-In
→ Dock Assignment
→ Loading/Unloading
→ Departure

The platform should become the industry's easiest and fastest way for drivers and warehouses to coordinate arrivals.

---

# Product Vision

Create the "Uber of Warehouse Check-In."

The experience should be:

* Extremely simple for drivers
* Extremely efficient for warehouses
* Mobile-first
* Event-driven
* Geofence-aware
* Enterprise scalable

The goal is to reduce:

* Driver waiting
* Manual check-ins
* Phone calls
* Radio communication
* Clipboard processes
* Gate congestion
* Dock delays
